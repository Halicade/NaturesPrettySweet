**Volcanic Fields** - TKKN_VolcanicFlow - Warm - tropical, lava, rich soil, steam vents. Inspired by Hawaiʻi Volcanoes National Park

**Redwood Forest** - TKKN_RedwoodForest - Cold - large forest with beautiful redwood trees. Rain, fog is often. Inspired by Redwood National Park, California.

**Tallgrass prairie** - TKKN_Grasslands - WarmArid - high winds, bright sun, droughts are common. Inspired by Great Plains, Central US

**Savanna** - TKKN_Savanna - Temperate - similar to grasslands. More trees, droughts, wildfires common

**Salt fields** - TKKN_Desert - WarmArid - Desert with large amounts of salt deposits. Can find salt weed. Inspired by Death Valley California

**Desert oasis** - TKKN_Oasis - WarmArid - found in extreme deserts. Has rich soil around Oasis but is otherwise barren. Inspired by big bend national Park
